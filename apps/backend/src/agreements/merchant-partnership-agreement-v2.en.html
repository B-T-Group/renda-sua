The Parties agree as follows:

1. Purpose

This Agreement sets out the terms under which the Merchant may offer, promote, and sell products and/or services through the Rendasua Platform, and the Parties' reciprocal obligations regarding commission, billing, delivery, confidentiality, and personal data protection. It applies to product/service sales and to vehicle or equipment rentals offered by the Merchant on the Platform, according to the service tier chosen below.

3. Service tiers

Rendasua offers three cumulative service tiers applicable to product/service sales and to vehicle/equipment rentals. Each tier includes the benefits of the previous tier. The Merchant may upgrade or return to a prior tier at any time, without penalty, upon written request to the Platform.

Tier Commission Included benefits
Standard {{standardCommission}} % Customer support, product/listing publishing, secure payment, seller dashboard, basic analytics. No setup fee and no volume or duration commitment.
Premium {{premiumCommission}} % All Standard benefits, plus better listing visibility, platform promotion, advanced analytics, and priority support.
Elite {{eliteCommission}} % All Premium benefits, plus homepage placement, personalized support, dedicated marketing campaigns, and a dedicated account manager.

The commission applicable to the Merchant is the rate for the tier selected in the Platform (default Standard unless upgraded).

4. Billing, commission, and payment terms

  • No activation fee and no recurring subscription or equipment rental fee is charged to the Merchant.
  • Commission is charged by Rendasua only on confirmed transactions (sale or rental actually completed); no commission is due on listings that do not result in a transaction.
  • Rendasua collects payment from the end customer through its secure payment system, retains the applicable commission, and remits the net balance to the Merchant according to the agreed payout schedule and banking details on file.
  • Compensation of delivery agents assigned to the Merchant's orders is entirely borne by Rendasua, with no additional charge to the Merchant.
  • Inventory synchronization with the Platform is automatic and real-time via the integrated stock management system.
  • Moving to a higher or lower tier takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle, without penalty.

5. Merchant obligations

  • Provide accurate, complete, and up-to-date information at registration and throughout the Agreement, and notify Rendasua promptly of any change.
  • Describe products, services, vehicles, or equipment faithfully, including availability, condition, and price.
  • Honour confirmed orders and reservations within agreed timelines and maintain customer-service standards consistent with the Platform.
  • Hold and maintain all permits, licences, registrations, and insurance required by law for the business and, where applicable, for vehicle or equipment rental.
  • Comply with applicable laws, including consumer protection, taxation, and personal data protection.
  • Refrain from circumventing the Platform to solicit, outside Rendasua, customers generated by the Platform during the term of this Agreement.
  • Use packaging that can identify Rendasua INC. where applicable.

6. Rendasua obligations

  • Provide the Merchant with Platform access, a seller dashboard, and tools corresponding to the chosen tier.
  • Ensure secure payment processing and remittance of amounts due according to the agreed terms.
  • Coordinate and compensate the delivery network assigned to the Merchant's orders.
  • Provide customer support and, depending on the tier, personalized guidance, advanced analytics, or a dedicated account manager.
  • Protect the confidentiality of the Merchant's commercial and personal information in accordance with this Agreement.

7. Term and termination

This Agreement enters into force on the date of electronic acceptance by the Merchant and remains in force for an indefinite term, with no minimum volume or duration commitment on the Standard tier. Either Party may terminate at any time, without penalty, with at least fifteen (15) days' written notice. Rendasua may suspend or terminate the Merchant's account immediately, without notice, in case of serious breach, fraud, misrepresentation, or violation of applicable law. Termination does not affect settlement of commissions and amounts due for transactions confirmed before the termination date.

8. Confidentiality

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including financial, commercial, and operational data, customer lists, commercial and pricing strategies, personal data of customers and users, source code, technology architecture and internal Platform processes, and the terms of this Agreement. Each Party shall keep the other's Confidential Information strictly confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent except as required by law or court order. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for two (2) years.

9. Personal data protection

The Parties shall process personal data collected or exchanged under this Agreement in accordance with applicable privacy laws (including, where relevant, Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector and Law 25, and applicable federal laws). The Merchant shall implement reasonable security measures to protect customer personal data accessed through the Platform and use it only as strictly necessary to fulfil orders.

10. Intellectual property

The Rendasua brand, logo, application, website, and Platform technology remain the exclusive property of Rendasua (Groupe B&T). The Merchant retains ownership of its brand, products, and uploaded content, and grants Rendasua a non-exclusive licence to use that content solely for promotion and operation of the Platform.

11. Liability and indemnification

The Merchant remains solely responsible for the quality, conformity, and safety of products, services, vehicles, or equipment offered on the Platform. Each Party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its breach, negligence, or violation of law.

12. Force majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform resulting from a force majeure event — an unforeseeable and irresistible event beyond its reasonable control.

13. Amendment

This Agreement may be amended only in writing by mutual agreement, except for updates to the Platform's general terms of use and privacy policy, which Rendasua may modify with reasonable notice to the Merchant.

14. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Québec and the applicable federal laws of Canada. Any dispute arising from this Agreement falls under the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the judicial district of Joliette, Québec.
