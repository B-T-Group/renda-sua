# InVideo briefs — Cameroon ads

Create **one brief per video**. In the New brief popup, paste **Brief name** and **One-line description**. After Create, paste the longer **Brief body** into the brief.

Market: Cameroon (Douala or Yaoundé). Language: French. Length: 20–30s.

---

## 1. Client — office lunch pickup

**Brief name:** CM client — office lunch pickup

**One-line description:** Busy exec orders lunch on Rendasua, restaurant preps it, he picks up with no queue and no change.

**Brief body:**

20–30s French ad, Cameroon (Douala or Yaoundé). Persona: client. Lead: Jean as a busy office executive.

Story: At the office, he is hungry and short on time. He orders food on Rendasua and pays in the app. Cut to Chef Paul’s restaurant: order received, food prepared, packed. Jean gets a “ready for pickup” notification, walks in, shows the order, takes the bag, leaves. He did not wait in line. He did not wait for change — already paid.

CTA: Commandez. Récupérez. C’est prêt. / Téléchargez l’app.

Tone: fast, clean, professional. Not delivery in this video — pickup only.

## 2. Merchant — home stock, paid orders

**Brief name:** CM merchant — home stock, paid orders

**One-line description:** She sells from home, now found online; orders arrive already paid, less hassle.

**Brief body:**

20–30s French ad, Cameroon. Persona: merchant. Lead: Nadia.

Story: She has stock at home (clothes, cosmetics, or groceries). Before: only people who know her. Now: her products are on Rendasua, discoverable. An order comes in — the client has already paid. She packs with less stress, no chasing money, no haggling at the door. Maybe a rider picks up the parcel.

CTA: Créez votre boutique gratuitement. Vos clients paient en ligne.

Tone: relief, pride, practical. Not a warehouse. Not unpaid cash-on-delivery.

## 3. Agent — extra cash, Mobile Money

**Brief name:** CM agent — extra cash, Mobile Money

**One-line description:** Young guy with no job delivers from time to time and gets paid by Mobile Money — self-employed.

**Brief body:**

20–30s French ad, Cameroon. Persona: agent. Lead: Eric, young man, ~18–25.

Story: He has nothing to do, no job. On Rendasua he can accept a delivery when there is one nearby. He picks up an order, delivers it (moto-taxi or local transport). He gets paid by Mobile Money. He is self-employed: his time, his choice, extra cash when there is a job — not a full-time uniform job.

CTA: Devenez agent. Gagnez en Mobile Money, à votre rythme.

Tone: hope, independence, real streets. Not a child. Not a corporate courier.
